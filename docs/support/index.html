Delete your cloud account and data

In TerraWarden 1.9.18 and later, you can permanently delete this device’s cloud sharing account and associated cloud data in the app. You do not need to email support for normal deletion.

  1. Connect to the internet. Finish saving or discard any current route recording.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy → Cloud Account & Data. You can also open this page from Layer Sync → Privacy.
  3. Read what will be removed, tap Delete Cloud Account and Data, then confirm Delete Account and Cloud Data.
  4. Keep the app connected until it confirms the server has accepted your request. Reopen the same page and use Check Deletion Status to check progress. Completion is shown as Cloud account deleted.

Once accepted, deletion continues automatically even if you close the app. It normally takes a few minutes; allow up to 24 hours. A request that is still being submitted has not yet been accepted, and an accepted request is not yet confirmation that deletion has finished.

This cannot be undone and affects shared groups. Every group you own is deleted for all members, including its invitations and shared layers, pins, routes and lines. In other groups, shared layers, pins, routes and lines you created or edited are removed. Removing a contributed layer also removes its contents, including content added by other members. Your memberships and displayed member name are removed too.

After acceptance, the app removes shared layers, pending uploads and saved sharing credentials from this device. Downloaded PDF maps, personal layers and app preferences remain. Sharing stays off until you explicitly enable Layer Sharing again in Layer Sync, which creates a new cloud account after deletion is complete.

Exported files and independent copies held by other people are outside this deletion. Read the privacy policy’s retention and deletion details for remaining technical records and retention periods.

If the service is unavailable or the request has not been accepted, reconnect and reopen this page so the app can submit it. If deletion is still pending after 24 hours, check the status while connected and contact support with the error message and your app version. Do not send passwords, invitation codes or deletion receipts.

Common questions

Can I use TerraWarden offline?

Imported maps and saved field data can be used on your device without an internet connection. Cloud layer sharing and satellite imagery searches or downloads need an internet connection. Pending shared changes can sync when you reconnect and use the app.

Why isn’t my location showing?

Check that Location Services are enabled and TerraWarden has permission in your device’s Settings. A usable location also depends on your device and reception. You can choose the satellite map area manually if a location is unavailable.

Why are my shared changes still pending?

Check your connection, make sure Layer Sharing is enabled, and open Layer Sync to try Sync Now. Confirm that you still have access to the group and permission to edit its layers.

How detailed is the satellite imagery?

TerraWarden creates maps from public Sentinel-2 visual imagery at 10 metres per pixel. The imagery date and cloud coverage vary by acquisition. Choose an available acquisition that suits your selected area.

How do I delete my cloud account and data?

In TerraWarden 1.9.18 or later, open Settings → Privacy → Cloud Account & Data. Read the effects on shared groups and contributed content, then confirm deletion. Stay connected until the request is accepted and reopen the page to check completion. Downloaded PDFs and personal layers remain on your device. See the full deletion steps.

Why is the cloud deletion button unavailable?

You need an internet connection and a cloud account stored on this device. Finish saving or discard any current route recording first. If no account is stored, the page says so; you do not need to create an account just to use local maps. If deletion is already in progress, use the status page to check it.

Why do I need new sharing groups after updating to 1.9.18?

Version 1.9.18 starts fresh cloud sharing. Old shared groups and invitation codes are retired, and the app clears old shared caches and pending changes when you upgrade. All group members need to update to 1.9.18 or later, then create or join fresh groups using new invitation codes. Downloaded PDF maps and personal layers stay on your device.

Does deleting the app delete shared cloud data?

No. Removing TerraWarden removes its local app-container data, but it does not by itself delete the cloud sharing account or cloud records. Use in-app cloud account deletion before uninstalling if you want to remove that account and its associated cloud data.
