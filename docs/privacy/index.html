How TerraWarden works

TerraWarden is a field-mapping app that can store imported PDF maps, pins, drawn lines, measurements and recorded GPS routes on your device. Cloud layer sharing and satellite map creation are optional features.

TerraWarden does not use advertising or usage analytics. Optional sharing uses Google Firebase services.

Maps and data on your device

PDF maps you select for import are copied into the app’s storage. Local field data can include pin and layer names, coordinates, notes, site information, unit IDs, timestamps, lines and routes. Draft recordings, document-bound annotations, map preferences and appearance settings are also stored locally.

Layer sharing does not upload your PDF map files. Depending on your Apple device settings, local app data may also be included in device backups managed by Apple.

Location and route recording

With your permission, TerraWarden uses Location Services and compass heading to show your position, direction, GPS accuracy and map coordinates. The satellite map creator can also use your current location to help select an area.

If you start recording a route, location recording can continue while the app is in the background. Route data can include coordinates, times, accuracy and available movement information. Pause or stop the recording to end that recording session.

You can change location permissions in your device’s Settings. Functions that do not require GPS can still be used without a location fix.

Optional cloud layer sharing

When you enable layer sharing or create or join a sync group, the app can create an anonymous Firebase Authentication account for your device. This does not require an email address or password, but the account has a persistent identifier.

Shared data can include pins, geographic routes and lines, names, notes, coordinates, timestamps, unit and site metadata, group membership, an optional display name, and account/device identifiers used to synchronize changes.

Google Firebase processes this data to provide authentication, synchronization and cloud account deletion. Account identifiers also link owned groups and shared contributions to the account so they can be removed when deletion is requested. Authorized members of your group can receive shared content and retain offline copies. Group owners control membership and edit permissions. Keep invitation codes private.

Disabling sharing pauses synchronization; it does not itself erase data already stored by the service or copies already received by others.

Satellite imagery services

When you search for satellite imagery, the selected geographic bounding box and search period are sent to Element 84’s Earth Search service. Imagery is downloaded from Amazon Web Services-hosted assets and processed on your device to create the map.

Those services receive the network information needed to handle the requests, such as your IP address. If you center the selection on your GPS location, the selected area may reveal your approximate whereabouts. You can choose an area manually instead.

Import, export and sharing files

TerraWarden accesses files you choose through the system file picker or share sheet. When you export or share a PDF or KML file, its contents may include location data and field metadata. The destination you choose controls the exported copy under its own terms and privacy practices.

Removing an item from TerraWarden does not remove files that you exported elsewhere or independent copies held by recipients.

Retention and deletion

Delete your cloud account in the app

In TerraWarden 1.9.18 and later and TerraWarden Field 1.0 and later, open Settings → Privacy → Cloud Account & Data to request permanent deletion of this device’s cloud account and associated cloud data. The same page is available from Layer Sync → Privacy. Read the explanation, select Delete Cloud Account and Data, then confirm. Normal deletion does not require contacting support.

The confirmation explains the effects on other group members. Deletion removes:

  • Every sync group the account owns, with its invitations, shared layers, pins, routes, lines and stored route geometry. These groups are removed for all members.
  • The account’s memberships and displayed member name in other groups.
  • Shared layers, pins, routes and lines the account created or edited in other groups. Removing a contributed layer removes its contents, including content added by other members.
  • Unfinished cloud route uploads, records linking the account to its groups and contributions, and the Firebase Authentication account.

Request acceptance and completion

An internet connection is needed to submit the request. Keep the app connected until the server confirms acceptance. Once accepted, the server continues deletion automatically if the app closes and retries interrupted work. It normally completes within a few minutes; allow up to 24 hours.

The app shows submission, pending and completed status. An accepted request does not mean deletion is complete. Reopen Cloud Account & Data and use Check Deletion Status to check progress; completion is shown as Cloud account deleted. Field data and the Authentication account are removed before completion is recorded.

Data on your device

After acceptance, the app removes shared layers, pending uploads and saved sharing credentials from this device. Downloaded PDF maps, personal layers and app preferences remain. Sharing stays off until you explicitly enable it again; using Layer Sharing again after completion creates a new cloud account.

You can delete imported maps and personal field items using the app’s controls. Removing the app removes data in its app container. Uninstalling the app, pausing sharing or leaving a group does not by itself delete the cloud account and its associated cloud data. Device backups managed by Apple have their own retention arrangements.

Ordinary item deletion

Cloud sharing records are retained to operate and synchronize shared groups. Deleting an individual item or group can leave synchronization removal records, associated metadata or stored route data. Use cloud account deletion above to remove the account and associated cloud data; pausing sharing does not purge these records.

Technical records after cloud account deletion

A random deletion receipt allows status checks for 30 days. Its backend record contains only status and timestamps and is removed at the next scheduled cleanup after that period. The receipt cannot sign in or access field content.

A private account block record is kept for two hours after completion and removed at the next scheduled cleanup. It prevents already-issued sign-in tokens from recreating data. Scheduled cleanup runs every 15 minutes.

Permanent removal records contain random record IDs without account identifiers, names, coordinates or notes. They prevent stale offline devices from restoring erased data.

Independent copies and other retention

Files exported or independently copied by recipients remain under their control. Provider security logs and any separately configured backups have their own retention arrangements and are outside the automated deletion process.

This website and support email

These webpages are hosted by GitHub Pages. The site does not include advertising or third-party analytics scripts. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed through the developer’s email service to respond to your enquiry. Please send only information needed to explain the issue.

Security and service providers

The app uses encrypted HTTPS connections for its cloud-sharing and imagery requests. Sharing credentials are stored in the device’s Keychain, and group access is governed by service permissions. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google/Firebase, Element 84, Amazon Web Services, GitHub and the email provider process information associated with the features described above under their applicable terms and privacy practices. Their infrastructure may process information outside your country.

Your choices and contacting us

You can control location permission, pause sharing, manage group access according to your role, delete local data, use the in-app cloud account deletion controls, and choose whether to export files. For questions about access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with TerraWarden, contact luker313@icloud.com.
